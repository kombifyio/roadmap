# Privacy in the kombify community hub

This is a public GitHub repository. Discussions, issues, comments and votes
can be visible together with your GitHub profile and contribution history.
Other people can copy or index public content. Do not post passwords,
tokens, private access links, customer records or other personal information.

kombify uses community contributions to understand requests, answer
questions and moderate the community. Private product feedback stays
private. When it informs a public idea, kombify writes that idea in its own
words; private submissions are not copied or quoted here. For account
support, private feedback or a security report, use the routes in
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

GitHub operates the hosting and account features and processes account,
content and usage data under its
[Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
GitHub describes its international processing, including transfers to the
United States, in that statement's
[international data transfers section](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement#international-data-transfers).
Using this hub does not mean your contribution stays in the European Union.

You can edit or delete your contributions using GitHub's controls. For a
privacy question or a request concerning content kombify manages, contact
[info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io). Copies made by other people or search engines may
remain after the original content changes. GitHub's statement explains the
rights and controls for data GitHub processes.
